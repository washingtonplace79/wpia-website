Join your neighbors for the WPIA's annual meeting. As in past years, Ben and Liz Dressel have graciously offered the upper room of Dressel's Public House. **The WPIA will pay for your first drink from the bar** — please consider booking dinner before or after the meeting to support Dressel's.

## On the agenda

- A year in review and the state of the neighborhood after the May 2025 tornado
- **Q&A with Alderman Michael Browning**
- Tree questions — we're working to bring a representative from the City
- Annual dues collection ($75/household) — you can pay at the meeting or online
- **Open board roles**: President, Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary all serve one-year terms. We need volunteers — raise your hand when we ask!

## Annual dues

Dues are $75/year. Between maintaining the Traffic Circle and the gate, hosting social events, and the major work needed to restore the Circle after the tornado, your dues genuinely matter this year.

You can pay at the meeting, or in advance online via PayPal, Apple Pay, or credit card — see the **Pay Your Dues** button on the [Contact page](/contact/).
