Join your neighbors for our annual spring clean-up! This is a great way to get outside, meet people, and take pride in our shared spaces.

## What we'll be doing

- Cleaning up common areas and the north greenway
- Planting seasonal flowers along the main entrance
- Refreshing the playground mulch at Greenway Park
- Picking up litter along the neighborhood streets

## What to bring

Gloves, rakes, and any gardening tools you have. Water, coffee, and light snacks will be provided. All ages are welcome — children are encouraged to participate with a parent or guardian.

Meet at the **Pavilion at 9:00 AM**. Rain date is April 26th.

Questions? Email board@washingtonplace.org.
