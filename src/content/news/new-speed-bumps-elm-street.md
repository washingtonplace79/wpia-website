After months of discussion and a resident survey showing strong support, the city has officially approved the installation of two new speed bumps on Elm Street between Oak Avenue and Maple Drive.

## Background

The Traffic Committee has been working with the city's Department of Public Works for over a year to address chronic speeding concerns on Elm Street. A survey conducted in January 2026 showed 78% of Elm Street residents in favor of traffic-calming measures.

## Installation timeline

Installation is scheduled to begin in late April and will take approximately two to three days. Residents on Elm Street will receive a direct notice by mail at least one week before work begins.

During installation, a brief lane closure may be necessary. The city will provide adequate advance notice for any temporary detours.

## Next steps

The Traffic Committee will continue monitoring traffic speeds after installation. If you have questions or concerns, contact the committee at traffic@washingtonplace.org.
