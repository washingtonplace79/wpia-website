Minutes from the March 5th regular board meeting are now available in the [Documents](/documents/) section of this website.

## Key topics covered

**Budget review** — The treasurer presented the Q1 financial summary. The association is currently tracking within 3% of the approved annual budget.

**Summer event planning** — The board discussed preliminary plans for the annual neighborhood picnic in July and the back-to-school block party in August. A volunteer sign-up will be posted in May.

**Fence repair proposal** — The board reviewed three contractor bids for the perimeter fence repair along the north greenway. A decision will be made at the April meeting after one additional bid is received.

**Community garden update** — The garden committee reported that plots are fully reserved for the 2026 season and a waitlist has been started.

The next regular board meeting is **Monday, April 7th at 7:00 PM** in Community Center Room 2. All residents are welcome to attend.
