Washington Place is growing! Please join us in giving a warm welcome to two families who recently joined our neighborhood.

**The Martinez family** moved into 412 Birch Lane at the end of February. They have two kids, ages 7 and 10, and a very friendly golden retriever named Biscuit.

**The Chen family** moved into 418 Birch Lane in mid-February. They are looking forward to getting settled in and meeting their neighbors.

## A note from the Welcome Committee

If you live near Birch Lane, we encourage you to stop by and introduce yourself over the coming weeks — a friendly face goes a long way! The Welcome Committee has already delivered welcome packets with neighborhood information, upcoming event details, and contact information for the board.

If you'd like to join the Welcome Committee or help deliver welcome packets in the future, reach out to us at welcome@washingtonplace.org.
