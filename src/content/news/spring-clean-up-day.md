Join your neighbors for our annual spring clean-up! This is a great opportunity to spruce up our shared spaces and get to know your fellow residents.

## What we'll be doing

- Cleaning up the common areas and green spaces along the main entrance
- Planting seasonal flowers in the entrance bed
- Refreshing the playground mulch at Greenway Park
- Picking up litter along the neighborhood streets

## What to bring

Bring gloves, rakes, and any gardening tools you have on hand. Water, coffee, and light snacks will be provided. All ages and skill levels are welcome — many hands make light work!

Meet at the Pavilion at **9:00 AM**. We expect to wrap up by 1:00 PM. Rain date is April 26th.

Questions? Email board@washingtonplace.org.
